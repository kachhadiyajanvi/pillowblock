SECTOR OVERVIEW

Extreme Performance for Mining Operations

Mining environments represent the ultimate test for rotational components. Pillow block bearings for mining equipment must survive constant exposure to abrasive dust, heavy vibration, and massive shock loads. CNZ Bearings delivers heavy-duty solutions engineered to keep high-capacity hoists and massive conveyors moving in the world's most demanding extraction sites.

Our mining-series units are designed with advanced triple-lip seals and reinforced cast iron housings to prevent particulate ingress. By focusing on material purity and precise heat treatment, we ensure that our bearings can handle the extreme radial and axial loads common in crushing and screening machinery.

Downtime in a mining operation is measured in thousands of dollars per minute. Choosing CNZ's pillow block bearings for mining ensures a longer MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures) and reduced maintenance cycles, even in deep underground or high-altitude open-pit mines.

Why CNZ Bearings for Mining

Extreme Load Resilience

High-capacity internal geometry designed to withstand the massive static and dynamic loads of primary crushers and ball mills.

Superior Dust Sealing

Multi-stage labyrinth seals prevent fine mineral dust and grit from compromising the internal rolling elements in harsh dry mines.

Shock Load Absorption

Ductile iron housings provide high tensile strength to absorb violent impact loads during raw material screening and sorting.

Moisture Protection

Specialized high-viscosity grease and plating options protect against corrosion in wet-process mines and underground seepage.
